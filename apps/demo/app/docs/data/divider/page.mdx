## Divider

A Divider separates items in a list with a thin line. This is used when
separation cannot be accomplished with Cards or white space.

<DemoCard
  component={Divider}
  initialProps={{
    className: "w-full",
  }}
/>

```tsx
<Divider />
```

### Props

- `StyleableProps` are available: `className` and `style`.
- `ElementCustomizableProps` are available: `element`.
