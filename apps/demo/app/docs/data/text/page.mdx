## Text

A piece of text with a typographic style applied.

```tsx
<Text type="headline-large">The quick brown dog jumps over the lazy fox.</Text>
```

### Props

- `StyleableProps` are available: `className` and `style`.
- `ElementCustomizableProps` are available: `element`.
