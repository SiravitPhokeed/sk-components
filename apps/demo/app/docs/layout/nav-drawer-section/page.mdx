## Nav Drawer Section

A group of destinations within a Navigation Drawer.

  material="/components/navigation-drawer/overview"

<DemoCard

      <NavDrawerItem

```tsx
<NavDrawerSection header="Search">
  <NavDrawerItem
    icon={<MaterialIcon icon="book" />}
    label="Subjects"
    selected
  />
  <NavDrawerItem icon={<MaterialIcon icon="groups" />} label="Classrooms" />
</NavDrawerSection>
```

### Props

- [`StyleableProps`](/docs/guides/shared-props): `className` and `style`.
- [`ElementCustomizableProps`](/docs/guides/shared-props): `element`.
