## App Drawer

A drawer of related apps.

<DemoCard
  component={Button}
  initialProps={{
    children: "Apps",
    appearance: "text",
    icon: <MaterialIcon icon="apps" />,
  }}
/>

```tsx
<AppDrawer>
  <AppDrawerSegment header="School apps">
    <AppDrawerItem name="Main site" logo={<img src="/main.svg" />} />
    <AppDrawerItem name="MyLearn+" logo={<img src="/my-learn.svg" />} />
  </AppDrawerSegment>
</AppDrawer>
```
