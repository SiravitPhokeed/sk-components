## App Drawer Item

An app inside the App Drawer. A child of App Drawer Segment.

<DemoCard
  component={AppDrawerItem}
  initialProps={{
    name: "MyLearn+",
    logo: <div className="size-16 bg-linear-to-b from-surface-variant to-secondary-container" />,
  }}
/>

```tsx
<AppDrawerItem
  name="MyLearn+"
  logo={<img src="/my-learn.svg" />}
/>
```

### Props

- `ActionableProps` are available: `command`, `commandFor`, `onClick`, and `href`.
- `StyleableProps` are available: `className` and `style`.
- `ElementCustomizableProps` are available: `element`.
